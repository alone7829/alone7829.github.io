Privacy Policy - Pahadi Racer

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Pahadi Racer (package com.rkp.pahadiracer, "the app"), published by Rkp Studio ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. It applies to users worldwide, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States (including California) and India.

1. Data controller and contact

Rkp Studio, India. Email: rkprajapati155223@gmail.com. You can contact us about any privacy question or to exercise your rights.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The app has no login and no chat. Your game progress (coins, unlocked vehicles, upgrades and best distances) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not have access to it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect: advertising ID, device and operating system information, app interaction and ad interaction data, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and diagnostic data. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent. Users in these regions are shown a consent message (Google's certified consent management) before personalised ads are shown. You can refuse; you will then see non-personalised or limited ads. You can withdraw or change consent at any time by clearing the app data or reinstalling the app, which shows the consent message again. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security rely on our legitimate interest in funding and protecting a free app.

5. Your rights

EEA / UK / Switzerland: you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states: you have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Ad data shared with Google may be considered "sharing"; you can opt out by turning off ad personalisation on your device (Settings - Google - Ads - Delete advertising ID) or by contacting us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

India (DPDP Act 2023): you may request access, correction or erasure and raise grievances by email to the contact above.

Because we do not store personal data on our servers, most requests are handled by Google; we will help you route them. We reply within 30 days.

6. International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Retention

We retain no personal data. Google retains advertising data according to its own policy.

8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Data sent to ad services is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS). Game progress stays on your device.

10. Permissions

The app uses internet access and the advertising ID permission only for ads. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, storage or precise location.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
