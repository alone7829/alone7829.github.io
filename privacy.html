Privacy Policy - Pahadi Racer

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Pahadi Racer ("the app"), published by Rkp Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location. The app has no login and no chat. Your game progress (coins, unlocked vehicles, upgrades and best distances) is stored only on your own device. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device advertising identifier, device model, operating system version, general location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. This information is used to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where allowed. We do not receive or store this information ourselves. Google's use of data is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can limit ad personalisation on your device at any time in Settings - Google - Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Permissions

The app uses the internet permission only to load advertisements. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, storage or precise location.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share any information with third parties other than the advertising service described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
